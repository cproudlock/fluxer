Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

Summary

We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. In short:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not train AI models on your messages, files, or any other content you create or share on Echowire.
  • The service is not end-to-end encrypted, but we use strong encryption for data in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • We aim to collect only the minimum data necessary to provide, secure, and improve the service.
  • You can export, manage, and delete your data through your account settings and privacy dashboard.
  • We handle personal data in line with applicable privacy laws, including GDPR and CCPA/CPRA, based on your location.
  • We log limited feature usage and operational events to keep the service reliable and secure. We do not use this data for behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking.

This summary is provided for convenience. Please read the full policy below.

1. Who We Are

Echowire is a self-hosted instance of the Fluxer chat platform, independently operated at echowire.org. We are not affiliated with Fluxer Platform AB. When this policy refers to "Echowire", "we", "us", or "our", it refers to the operator of this Echowire instance.

As the operator of this instance, we act as the data controller for your personal data when you use Echowire, meaning we determine how and why your personal data is processed.

We comply with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

You can find our contact details in Section 16.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in three main ways: (1) information you provide to us, (2) information we collect automatically, and (3) information we receive from other sources.

2.1 Information You Provide to Us

  • Account information: Email address, username, password (stored using Argon2id, a modern secure password hashing algorithm), and birth year. During registration, we collect your full date of birth to verify you meet the minimum age requirement for your region (COPPA/regional compliance). After verification, we retain only your birth year - the month and day are discarded and never stored. Optionally, your phone number for two-factor authentication (2FA) where available.
  • Content and communications: Messages, files, images, voice communications (where supported), community information, reactions, and profile data (such as avatar, bio, and other information you choose to display). This also includes information about communities you create or administer.
  • Support and correspondence: Information you provide when you contact us for support, including the content of messages and any additional details you choose to provide.
  • Payment information: If you purchase premium features, payment processing is handled securely by Stripe. We do not store your full payment card details. Stripe provides us with limited information necessary to manage your purchases (for example, billing country, partial card details, payment status, and timestamps).

We do not require you to provide special categories of personal data (such as information about your health, religion, or political beliefs). If you choose to share such information in your messages or profile, you do so at your own discretion.

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you use Echowire, we automatically collect certain technical and usage information, including:

  • Device and technical information: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, device identifiers, language settings, and similar technical data.
  • Usage information: Information about how you interact with Echowire, such as pages and screens visited, features used, timestamps and duration of sessions, approximate counts of events (for example, messages sent, communities joined) without reading message content, and crash reports and performance metrics.
  • Security and operational logs: Login attempts and authentication events, changes to account settings, rate limits, API errors, and IP-based signals related to spam, abuse, or unusual behavior.
  • Push notification tokens: Device tokens for delivering mobile push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). These tokens identify your device for notification delivery only.

We use this information to secure the service, detect and prevent abuse, improve performance, and understand which features are being used. We do not use this information for behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking.

2.3 Information From Other Sources

We may also receive information about you from:

  • Other users: When other users mention you, add you to communities, send you messages, or otherwise interact with your account.
  • Service providers: Limited operational information from our service providers, such as payment confirmations from Stripe, or transactional email delivery records from smtp2go.

4. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

4.1 When You Direct Us to Share

We share your information when you intentionally interact with others on Echowire, for example when you send messages, join or participate in communities, share content, or when your profile information is visible to others according to your settings.

4.2 With Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate Echowire. Here is an honest accounting of every third-party service your data may touch:

Infrastructure and Content Delivery

  • Cloudflare — Content delivery network (CDN), web application firewall (WAF), and DDoS protection. Cloudflare sits in front of our servers, so your requests pass through their network. Cloudflare also provides Turnstile CAPTCHA for bot prevention. Cloudflare R2 is used for file storage (attachments, avatars, media).

Voice and Real-Time Communication

  • LiveKit — Voice server software running on our own dedicated servers across 5 US regions. LiveKit handles voice channel audio. The servers are ours; LiveKit is the open-source software running on them. Your voice data flows through these servers but is not stored or recorded.

Payments

  • Stripe — Payment processing for premium subscriptions. We do not store your full payment card details. Stripe processes and stores payment information under their own privacy policy and PCI compliance.

Email

  • smtp2go — Transactional email delivery (account verification, password resets, security alerts). smtp2go sees the recipient email address and email content for the messages we send.

Push Notifications

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — Push notification delivery for Android and iOS apps. FCM receives a device token and notification payload (which may include the sender name and a preview of the message) to deliver notifications to your device. FCM is operated by Google.

Third-Party Content

  • Klipy — GIF search. All Klipy traffic is routed through our servers, so Klipy does not see your IP address or device identifiers.
  • YouTube — Video embeds. We fetch metadata server-side. YouTube only receives information from your device if you choose to play an embedded video.

Safety

  • Arachnid Shield API — CSAM scanning for user-uploaded media, operated by the Canadian Centre for Child Protection (C3P). See Section 5 for details.

Where required by law, we have data processing agreements in place with providers that process personal data on our behalf, and we take steps to minimize the amount of data shared with all providers.

5. Content Scanning for Safety

We use the Arachnid Shield API, operated by the Canadian Centre for Child Protection (C3P), to help identify and block child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in user-uploaded media.

  • How it works: When media is uploaded, the file is sent to the Arachnid Shield API over HTTPS for analysis. The API checks the file against C3P's database of known CSAM. This is hash-based matching only — it does not use AI or content analysis, and cannot detect content that has not been previously identified.
  • What is scanned: All user-uploaded media (avatars, emojis, stickers, file attachments).
  • What is not scanned: Text messages are not scanned by this tool.
  • When a match is detected: The upload is immediately rejected. We take appropriate action, which may include account suspension and reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as legally required.

We may also use automated systems to detect spam, malware, phishing, and other abusive content or behavior. These systems primarily rely on metadata and patterns, not the content of your private messages.

6. Data Storage and Security

6.1 Where Your Data Is Stored

Echowire is a self-hosted platform. Your data is stored on private infrastructure that we own and operate — not on shared cloud hosting platforms. Specifically:

  • Primary servers: Private dedicated servers located in the United States, running on hardware we control.
  • Database: ScyllaDB (Cassandra-compatible), replicated across multiple nodes on our private infrastructure for redundancy.
  • File storage: Cloudflare R2 for user-uploaded files and media.
  • Encrypted backups: Backups are encrypted using age encryption and stored on both Cloudflare R2 and private network-attached storage.

Because we control our own infrastructure, we have direct oversight of physical and logical access to your data. There is no multi-tenant cloud provider sitting between us and your data.

6.2 Security Measures

We implement the following measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption for data in transit (TLS) on all connections.
  • Encryption for data at rest on our servers and backups (age encryption for backups).
  • Firewall rules and network segmentation between services.
  • Mesh VPN (NetBird) for secure inter-node communication — services do not communicate over the public internet.
  • Regular security updates and infrastructure hardening.
  • Rate limiting and abuse detection.
  • Strict access controls with a minimal number of administrators.

No online service can guarantee perfect security. We work continuously to improve our security posture and reduce risks.

6.3 Data Breaches

If we become aware of a data breach that has a material impact on your personal data, we will investigate and take appropriate remedial steps, notify you without undue delay when legally required, and notify relevant supervisory authorities when required by law.

During registration, we collect your full date of birth to accurately verify age eligibility against your region's requirements. After this verification, we retain only your birth year - the month and day are immediately discarded and never stored. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the minimum age in their region. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child who does not meet the age requirement, we will take steps to delete that information and the account.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has used Echowire without your consent or does not meet the minimum age requirement, contact us at support@echowire.org to request deletion of their account.
